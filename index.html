College Must Haves

Laptop

A reliable laptop is essential for coding, running software, and completing assignments efficiently. It allows you to work on projects, attend online classes, and stay productive throughout your day.

Cellphone

A cellphone helps you stay connected, manage schedules, and access learning resources on the go. It’s also a handy tool for communication and coordinating group projects efficiently.

Charger or Powerbank

A reliable charger and power bank are essential to keep your devices running throughout the day. They ensure you can complete assignments, attend online classes, and stay productive without interruptions.

Cloud Storage

Cloud storage provides a secure way to save and access files from anywhere. It’s useful for backing up projects, collaborating with classmates, and keeping your work organized.
